How It Works

Data first.
Then a phone call.

01

Your buy box

Revenue range, margin floor, industries and states. We agree on a valuation metric and what reasonable diligence looks like before we source a single company.

One call to set it
02

The universe

Every company we can find that fits: state business registries, federal benefit-plan filings (a headcount signal), licensing data and market history on businesses that have been listed for sale.

Public records, not scraped lists
03

The screen

We cut the universe to companies that match on size, age, ownership and industry, and name the owner. Sold, PE-backed and already-listed companies are flagged or removed.

Owner-operated, founder-held
04

The outreach

On the Sourced plan we contact owners directly, quietly and under our name, and find out who would take a conversation in the next year or two.

No blast emails under your name
05

The introduction

You get owners who said yes to talking, with notes on why they might sell, rough numbers and timing. You decide who to meet.

Warm intros only
06

The match

We match on four things: the money is real, buyer and seller fit, both sides accept the valuation method, and diligence is reasonable. If one is missing, we say so early.

Fewer meetings, better ones
