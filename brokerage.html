The Flow

Six steps, one system,
no manual handoffs.

01

A buyer inquires

A lead arrives from BizBuySell or your own site. Routing reads which listing it’s for and sends it to that listing’s brand inbox.

Marketplace leads and your own forms
02

The NDA invite goes out

The buyer gets a personal email from your address with one link to sign. Replies come back to the same inbox.

Sent under your brand, not ours
03

The buyer signs online

One short form: contact details, budget and what they’re looking for. A binding e-signature, with date, IP and exact terms recorded.

Non-circumvention included
04

You’re alerted

The moment the NDA is signed you get the buyer’s details, budget and reason for interest. The buyer is added to the CRM.

Every signed NDA on record, per listing
05

Access is granted

The buyer receives a private data-room link for that listing and can come back to it any time.

One private link per buyer, per deal
06

The deal room does the first round

CIM and financials in one place. The Deal Concierge answers routine questions, and every view is logged, so you know who’s serious.

Confidential, all access logged

Questions

What brokers ask.

Does it show our name or yours?

Yours. Emails go out from your brand’s address and replies come back to your inbox. Buyers never see Sosville.

Which marketplaces does routing work with?

BizBuySell today, plus inquiry forms on your own website. Tell us where else your leads come from.

Is the NDA enforceable?

Checking the box is a binding e-signature. The date, IP address and exact terms are recorded, and non-circumvention is included.

Who runs this?

Sosville. We built it for our own deals and run the same flow on our own listings every day.
