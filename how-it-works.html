How the system works, from first inquiry to closing.

Every way a buyer or seller reaches you feeds one system. It answers from the listing file and from what you've already said, learns from every reply you send, and hands you only the judgment calls. Here's each piece, in the order a deal moves through it.

  1. One lead queue
  2. Smart email
  3. It learns from you
  4. NDA flow
  5. Deal rooms
  6. Smart agent
  7. Seller due diligence
  8. Website and DealLedger
1

Every lead lands in one queue

Nothing gets lost between your inbox, the marketplaces and your website.

Everything that comes in is pulled into a single inbound queue, matched to the right listing, and attached to one record per buyer or seller:

  • Marketplace leads (BizBuySell and similar)
  • Emails to your inbox and your shared inbox
  • Website contact forms and site chat
  • NDA signatures
  • Questions asked inside a deal room

Each item is sorted by what it is: a buyer question, a request for deal room access, an offer, a seller who wants to list, a vendor, or spam. Offers and new seller leads go to the top.

Marketplace leads get an automatic first reply with the listing's NDA link, so a buyer can get into the deal room before you've seen the email.

2

Smart email drafts your replies

Open Gmail and the reply is already written, in your voice, in the right thread.

Every 15 minutes the system reads new buyer and seller emails and writes a reply as a draft in your Gmail, threaded under the original message. You read it, edit if you want, and press send. Nothing goes out on its own.

What it's allowed to say

  • Only approved facts for that listing. If a number isn't in the listing file, it says you'll get it from the seller. It never guesses.
  • NDA-level facts only to buyers who signed that listing's NDA. Everyone else gets the link to sign.
  • Never location addresses, merchant names, the seller's identity or contracts before an accepted offer.
  • Offers, price, terms and anything legal get a short acknowledgment and a flag that says you'll follow up personally.

It also knows the listing's status: if a deal is under contract or sold, it says so and offers similar listings. Each draft comes with a one-line note telling you what it couldn't answer.

3

It learns from every reply you send

You answer a question once. The system answers the next buyer who asks it.

Every 30 minutes it compares each draft with what you actually sent.

  • Your answers become knowledge. A fact you gave one buyer is available, for that listing, to the next one.
  • Your edits become style. How you greet people, how you sign off, which phone number you give and how short you keep it.
  • A scorecard tracks it. You can see how often drafts go out unchanged, by type of email.
  • Auto-send, one type at a timeNext
    When a type of reply goes out unchanged nearly every time, you can let that type send on its own. Offers, pricing and legal never do.
4

NDAs sign themselves and open the deal room

No emailing PDFs back and forth, and no buyer waiting a day for access.

The buyer signs the NDA at the bottom of the listing page, in about 30 seconds. The signature is tied to that listing and that buyer's record, and the deal room opens immediately.

If the buyer goes quiet

  • Day 1: a follow-up 24 hours after they first open the deal room
  • Day 4: a second note
  • Day 8: a last check-in

Each one is branded for the business it belongs to, carries an unsubscribe link, and can't double-send. Buyers who say they already signed get pointed to the right listing instead of being asked again.

5

One deal room per listing

The files, the answers and the activity for a listing, in one place buyers can reach any time.

  • Private, link-based access for buyers who signed that listing's NDA
  • The listing summary, CIM and documents, with uploads from you or the seller
  • Questions and answers, so every buyer sees what's already been asked
  • Who has opened the room and when, so you know which buyers are serious

A CIM can be drafted for you from the seller's documents, for you to review before it goes in the room.

6

A smart agent answers buyers, day or night

Buyers get answers at 11pm on a Sunday. You get the questions only you can answer.

Inside each deal room, the agent answers buyer questions from the same sources the email drafts use: the seller's answers in the listing file and your past answers.

  • Public facts for anyone; NDA-level facts only for buyers with access to that room
  • Answers labeled by where they came from: verified by you, or from the seller and not yet verified
  • Anything it can't answer becomes a question for you, and your answer goes into the file for the next buyer
  • Offers and negotiation always go to you

Questions buyers ask most are counted per listing, so you know which gaps in the file are costing you.

7

Sellers fill in their own due diligence

One link replaces weeks of chasing P&Ls and processor statements.

The seller gets a private page for their listing that works on a phone, with the questions buyers ask most at the top. Every checklist item gets one of three answers:

  • The answer, or a file (P&L, statements, contracts, photos)
  • Coming soon, with the date they'll have it
  • Won't provide, with an optional reason

Each listing gets a transparency score, plus a separate verified score for what you've checked. At 70% a badge can show on the listing page. Answers flow straight into the email drafts and the smart agent, so buyers get them without you in the middle.

8

Your website, and your listings on DealLedger

The front door buyers see, kept current without anyone updating it by hand.

  • A fast, mobile broker site with a page per listing, built to be found in search
  • Listing pages that pull from the same file as everything else, with the NDA sign-up and transparency badge built in
  • Your listings on DealLedger, the open public record of businesses for sale, with true days on market, at no cost

What stays with you

  • OffersEvery offer and every negotiation.
  • PricingValuation, terms and seller financing.
  • LegalAnything a lawyer should see.
  • RelationshipsThe calls that win the listing and close the deal.
