One link replaces the due diligence chase.

It works like a mortgage application. Nothing is left blank, and nobody on your team has to follow up item by item.

  1. 1

    Send the seller one link

    From your CRM, one click emails the seller a private page for their listing. No login, works on a phone, and they can come back to it any time.

  2. 2

    Every item gets an answer

    For each checklist item the seller picks one:

    • The answer, or a file
    • Coming soon, with a date
    • Won't provide
  3. 3

    The listing gets a score

    Answered items count fully, partial answers half, and "won't provide" a third. At 70% your listing page shows buyers a transparency badge.

  4. 4

    Buyers get answers without you

    The answers feed your deal room and draft your replies to buyer emails. NDA-level facts only go to buyers who have signed.
